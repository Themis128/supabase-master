# Partners

Apply to the Partners program to list your integration in our marketplace and
grow your business.

[Become a Partner](https://forms.supabase.com/partner)

## OAuth App Steps

1. Register App: An OAuth app first needs to be registered with Supabase
2. Add OAuth2 Support: Use the OAuth2 protocol to access a users organization or
   project
3. Receive Tokens: You'll receive a new access and refresh token
4. Control Projects: Use Supabase REST API to control projects and other
   settings

## Feature Blocks

- **Technical support**: Access technical support to back your integrations and
  customer projects.
- **Expand your ecosystem**: Offer your own products and services to Supabase
  customers.
- **Business growth**: Explore new revenue streams and growth potential.
- **Scale with us**: Scale automatically with the power of open-source Postgres
  technology.
